# Mental Health Trigger Journal

> **The Mental Health Trigger Journal** is a digital sanctuary—a thoughtful, database-driven web application designed to help individuals understand their emotional patterns, document specific triggers, and securely connect with mental health professionals for guided, personalized support.

---

## 🌿 The Core Vision

This application bridges the gap between daily emotional awareness and structured professional therapy. Our primary focus is translating complex clinical data (logs, moods, and events) into a **warm, supportive, and completely intuitive visual experience.**

The goal is twofold: To empower individuals to reflect on their own patterns through a **cozy and safe interface**, and to provide therapists with clean, actionable data to better support their clients.

---

## ✨ A Glimpse Inside

We have crafted three distinct, interconnected portals, each sharing a beautiful and cohesive dashboard aesthetic:

### 1️⃣ The Patient Sanctuary

* **Safe Haven Theme:** A comforting space using soft pastel greens, peaches, and cream tones. It’s designed to feel positive, clinical-free, and inviting.
* **Mood & Affirmatons:** Start the day by selecting a cute, interactive mood emoticon and receiving a warm, encouraging daily affirmation at the click of a button.
* **Visual Insights:** Patients can easily see their progress streak and understand their most frequent triggers through simplified, elegant trend charts.

### 2️⃣ The Therapist Console

* **Clinical Clarity:** A professional yet therapeutic view designed to manage patient care efficiently.
* **Proactive Care:** A color-coded "Risk Matrix" alerts therapists instantly to critical, high-risk journal entries requiring immediate attention.
* **Side-by-Side Review:** Therapists can examine a patient’s logged trigger, mood, and notes in one pane, while using a second pane to leave encouraging clinical feedback.

### 3️⃣ The Admin Command Center

* **System Health:** A high-level view of application performance, active users, and database latency.
* **Governance:** Comprehensive tools for managing user roles, monitoring system activity logs, and ensuring database security and backups.
